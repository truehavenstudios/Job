Privacy

Job Application Tracker by TrueHaven Studios – last updated October 2026.

The tracker runs entirely in your browser. Everything you enter (jobs, interviews, resume points, stories, offers) is stored on your device and – after you sign in with Google – in your own Google Drive. There is no account with us and no server of ours that receives your data – we cannot see it.

Sign in with Google

The full app asks you to sign in with Google so your job search is never lost. It then saves a copy of your tracker data as one file in your own Google Drive, in a hidden app folder (scope drive.appdata). The tracker uses this file only to keep your data backed up and in sync between your devices. It can only see this one file – not your other Drive files, your emails or your contacts. The data goes directly from your device to Google; we never receive, store, share or sell it, and it is not used for advertising or to train AI models. You can sign out any time in Settings and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete the saved file, open Google Drive › Settings › Manage apps › find the app › Options › Delete hidden app data. Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Emails and calendar

Emails are opened in your own mail app and calendar events are downloaded as a file or opened in Google Calendar by you – the tracker never sends anything on its own.
