Privacy

Job Application Tracker by TrueHaven Studios – last updated October 2026.

The tracker runs entirely in your browser. Everything you enter (jobs, interviews, resume points, stories, offers) is stored on your device. There is no account with us, and without sync nothing leaves your device.

Device sync (optional)

If you link a second device in Settings, your tracker data is locked (AES-256-GCM) on your device before it is sent. The sync server, a Cloudflare Worker with a small database, only ever stores scrambled data it cannot read, a random space ID, a version number and timestamps. The keys stay on your linked devices (and in the link or QR code you use to add a device). There is no account, no email and no password. We do not sell, share or use the data, and we cannot recover it for you. You can unlink a device or turn sync off and erase the server copy at any time in Settings › Your devices.

Emails and calendar

Emails are opened in your own mail app and calendar events are downloaded as a file or opened in Google Calendar by you – the tracker never sends anything on its own.
